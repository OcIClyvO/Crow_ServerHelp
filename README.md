# CROW SMP — Server History Logs
Welcome to the CROW SMP History Archive.
This section of the website is dedicated to recording the history of CROW SMP and preserving the events that have taken place throughout the server's existence. As the server continues to grow, more players arrive, new clans are created, old places are forgotten, and events become harder to remember. These logs exist to make sure the past is not lost.
The history of CROW SMP is not simply a collection of dates. It is the story of the people who have lived here, the places they created, the conflicts they started, the friendships they made, and the events that changed the server.

## The History Archive
Each history log records an event, period of time, player, clan, or discovery that is considered important to the history of the server. Whenever possible, the logs are arranged chronologically so that players can follow the development of the server from its earliest days to the present.
Some records may be detailed, while others may only contain a small amount of information. This is because not everything that happened was documented at the time.

## A History That Is Not Complete
These records should not be considered a perfect account of everything that has happened.
Some events were never recorded. Some information has been lost. Some stories were passed from player to player and changed over time. There may even be different versions of the same event.
Because of this, certain records may contain information that is uncertain or disputed. When possible, different accounts are kept rather than removing them.
The archive records what is known.
It does not always explain what is true.

## Discovering the Past
If you are exploring CROW SMP and discover something that seems connected to an older event, check the history logs. A building, name, location, or message may have a history that goes back much further than expected.
Something that appears unimportant today may have been extremely important in the past.
Likewise, something that everyone remembers may have a completely different story behind it.
The further you look into the history of CROW SMP, the more connections you may find.

## Keeping the Records
The server will continue to change, and new events will eventually become part of its history.
These logs will be updated as new information is discovered and confirmed. If an event was previously misunderstood, the record may be changed to reflect what is now known.
The goal is simple: preserve the history of CROW SMP so that future players can understand the world they have joined.
